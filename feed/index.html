Protect Yourself Against Fraudulent Payment Requests

The Estate of GK Kirima has received reports of individuals falsely claiming to represent the Kirima Rise programme.

Residents are reminded that all payments must be made only through the official payment channels published on this website. Estate representatives will never request payment through personal bank accounts or unofficial mobile payment numbers.

If you receive suspicious calls, messages or payment instructions:

  • Do not make any payment.
  • Request official identification.
  • Contact the Estate immediately using the details provided on the Contact page.
  •  

Protecting residents from fraud remains one of our highest priorities.

Survey and Plot Verification Activities Continue Across Njiru

Survey and verification teams continue to carry out field activities across various parts of the Kirima Rise project area.

These activities are an important part of the regularisation programme and help ensure that plot boundaries, resident information and supporting documentation accurately correspond with the Estate’s authenticated survey records.

Residents may notice authorised survey personnel working within their areas over the coming weeks. We kindly ask for your cooperation as these activities contribute to the successful processing of lawful title deeds.

Further updates will be published as additional zones complete the verification process
